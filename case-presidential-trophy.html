Case study 01 — Award design

The Presidential Award trophy

A winning trophy design for the Outstanding Development Partners Award — built around one idea: how far the work of small businesses can reach.

Client
DTI Calabarzon · Shared Service Facilities
Year
2024
Role
Trophy designer
Studio
LIKHA FabLab
Result
Contest winner
01 · The brief

A trophy for the people who help small businesses grow

The Presidential Awards and Recognition held a design contest for the trophy given to Outstanding Development Partners — the organizations that support the country’s micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs). The trophy had to speak for all of them: their reach, their growth and the communities behind them.

02 · The idea

Every part of the form carries a meaning

Instead of decorating a standard trophy, each element of the design was given a job to do.

The globeAt the core: the reach and influence of MSMEs across the world.
The rising linesRadiating outward: market penetration and sustained growth.
The encircling ringDiversity and collaboration — inclusive work, community development and care for the environment.
03 · Design

Modeled, rendered and documented for production

The trophy was developed in 3D, then presented as a full design sheet: hero renders plus front, side and top views, so the judges — and the fabricators — could see exactly how it would be built.

04 · Result

Chosen as the winning design

The design won the Presidential Awards and Recognition Trophy Design Contest for the Outstanding Development Partners Award. The finished trophy — a gold globe held inside a ring of rising slats, on a dark base — carries the concept from sketch to the hands of the awardees.
