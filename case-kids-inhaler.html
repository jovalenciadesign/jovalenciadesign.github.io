Case study 02 — Healthcare product design

A friendlier inhaler for kids

Three animal characters — a platypus, a bear and a giraffe — designed around one shared inhaler body, so a daily medicine feels less like a treatment and more like a companion.

Type
Product concept
Role
Product designer
Tools
SolidWorks
Output
3 characters · renders · motion studies
01 · The problem

A medical object children have to use every day

A standard inhaler looks clinical and unfamiliar. For a young child who needs it daily, that can make an important routine feel scary. The aim was to turn the inhaler into something a child would actually want to pick up.

02 · The idea

One body, many characters

Rather than three separate products, the design uses a single shared inhaler core with interchangeable character shells. Kids get to choose a character; the parts inside stay the same.

Friendly charactersA platypus, a bear and a giraffe, with soft rounded forms and big eyes.
Shared coreThe same canister, mouthpiece, cap and housing in every version.
Built-in functionEach character’s beak or snout doubles as the mouthpiece.
03 · Engineering

Showing how it comes apart

Motion studies rotate each character to show its form, then explode it into the shared inhaler parts — making the one-core, many-shells system clear at a glance.

04 · Outcome

A complete product concept

The project delivered a family of three characters on a single platform, with production-ready renders and animated motion studies that explain the assembly — a foundation for prototyping and testing with children and parents.
